## EN

**Eyebrow:** Partner-delivered reference programs

**Heading:** The operational model, in production with critical-infrastructure operators.

**Intro:** These programs were delivered by Skyline Communications, the company that builds
DataMiner, for its own customers. We cite them with Skyline's permission to show the
operational model at national scale.

### Entry 1

**Name:** Kordia
**Place:** New Zealand
**Who:** State-owned operator of national broadcast and emergency communications networks.
**Body:** Kordia's 24/7 network operations center ran on separate tools from many vendors.
DataMiner replaced them with one layer for monitoring, control and automation across more
than 5,000 devices, and 98% of Kordia's infrastructure is now provisioned on it.
**Link:** Read the story on skyline.be → https://skyline.be/skyline/user/kordia

### Entry 2

**Name:** TDF
**Place:** France
**Who:** Broadcast and telecom infrastructure operator with 8,600 commercialized sites.
**Body:** TDF consolidated a fragmented set of network operations tools into one system,
phased network by network; digital terrestrial television alone runs to 14,000 elements.
New equipment is now monitored in weeks instead of months.
**Link:** Read the story on skyline.be → https://skyline.be/skyline/user/tdf

**Footnote:** Figures as published by Skyline Communications.
