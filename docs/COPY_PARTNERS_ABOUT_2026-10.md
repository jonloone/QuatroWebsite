## PART-1. Partners (`/partners`)

**eyebrow:** Partners
**H1:** Deliver the operational picture in your market.
**sub:** You hold the client relationship and know the market. Quatro integrates and operates the
operational picture behind you, in your country, and the client owns it from day one.
**primary CTA:** Talk to our team → /contact/demo

### The channel (three tiers)
1. **The local partner.** Leads the client relationship and the proposal, and is the single contracting
   counterparty on the program.
2. **Quatro.** Integrates the systems, the sensing and the satellite picture, and operates the result in the
   country with the client's team.
3. **Technology partners.** The technology underneath: DataMiner xOps for the operational model and Klarety for
   earth observation. `[approve]` Technology partners are treated at a flat fee inside the engagement, so the
   client sees one program and one price.

### What Quatro brings a partner
- **Sensing:** the Quatro IoT & Edge Suite and OT communications, placed only where confirmed risk justifies it.
- **Security and evidence:** chain-of-custody records and the operational agents that assemble them.
- **Data integration:** the systems the client already runs, brought into one operational model.
- **In-country operation:** hosting, support in Spanish, hypercare and a forward-deployed engineer.
- **Proposal support:** scoping, the proof-of-value plan and the technical sections of the proposal.

### Active markets (§1e)
Ecuador · Colombia · Venezuela · Panama · Guyana · MENA (through a channel partner)

### Ideal partners
- **Local primes with past performance** in energy, environment, mining, public services or security programs.
- **Geospatial and engineering firms** that already work the territory and the plant.
- **Programs aligned with development banks**, where a consortium led by a local prime delivers in tranches.

### Ownership line
Every engagement leaves the client owning the data, the derived products and the operational model.

### CTA
- **H2:** Tell us about your market and the program you are pursuing.
- **primary:** Talk to our team → /contact/demo
- **microcopy:** Hosted in your country · Your client owns it from day one
